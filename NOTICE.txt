This module implements the in-network parts of the 3GPP/ETSI Lawful
Interception architecture for SD-Core: the X1 provisioning interface
(ETSI TS 103 221-1), the X2/X3 delivery framing (ETSI TS 103 221-2), the
TS 33.128 xIRI encoder, the intercept-task store and the LI PKI helpers.
